Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the TIT SIH Internal Hackathon.

All regular students enrolled in Diploma, B.Tech, or M.Tech branches of Tripura Institute of Technology (TIT) are eligible. Inter-college teams are not permitted.

For the campus Internal Hackathon, only 2 members are required to register, and teams can include up to 6 members. Having at least 1 female student member is strictly mandatory in every team.

Teams can choose any Problem Statement published on the official SIH portal (sih.gov.in/sih2026PS) across Software Edition, Hardware Edition, or Open Student Innovation.

The Institute SPOC nominates top teams based on internal review scores. A maximum of 50 Teams (45 Shortlisted + 5 Waitlisted) will be nominated to represent TIT at the national SIH 2026.

The Special Ignite Award is an exclusive discretionary honor providing a 1-Month OpenAI ChatGPT Go subscription. It will be awarded only if a standout, genuinely innovative solution is presented during evaluations; otherwise, it will not be awarded.

TIT SIH 2026 Internal Hackathon Rulebook

Official Handbook & Evaluation Guidelines • Institution Innovation Council (IIC)

Institute SPOC Nomination & SIH 2026 Policy

• ONLY students / teams selected in this Internal Hackathon can be registered and nominated for the national Smart India Hackathon 2026.

• The College SPOC nominates top teams based purely on Internal Hackathon results. A maximum of 50 Teams (45 Shortlisted + 5 Waitlisted) can participate in Smart India Hackathon 2026 from the Institute.

• Final 6-Member SIH Squad: While internal campus registration allows 2–6 members, each nominated team submitted onto the central SIH portal must strictly consist of 6 members (including the Team Leader + at least 1 female member).

1. Team Formation, Problem Statement & Eligibility

  • Problem Statement: Can be any problem statement from the official SIH 2026 PS repository on sih.gov.in (Software Edition, Hardware Edition, or Student Innovation / Open Theme).
  • Internal Hackathon Team Size: Only 2 members are required to participate, with flexibility to have up to a maximum of 6 members (including Team Leader).
  • Mandatory Female Member: At least 1 female student member is strictly mandatory in every team (central AICTE / Ministry of Education SIH directive).
  • Same College Eligibility: All team members must be active, regular enrolled students of Tripura Institute of Technology (TIT) (Diploma, B.Tech, or M.Tech). No inter-college teams are permitted.
  • Cross-Department Collaboration: Inter-branch, cross-departmental, and inter-year collaborations within TIT are warmly encouraged.
  • Single Team Registration: A student can register with only one team under one Problem Statement.

2. Evaluation Criteria & Scoring Weightage

Evaluation Criterion Weightage Key Indicators
Innovation & Novelty 30% Uniqueness of solution, creative approach, USP over existing market alternatives.
Technical Feasibility & Architecture 25% Robust tech stack choice, scalability, system workflow, API/hardware design.
Societal & Real-World Impact 20% Alignment with Ministry Problem Statement, user impact, sustainability.
Prototype / Demo Readiness 15% Working wireframes, GitHub repository, live API test or simulated demo.
Pitch & Presentation Quality 10% Adherence to standard 7-slide PPT template, clarity of answers during Q&A.

3. PPT Template Structure

All teams must strictly use the official 7-slide PPT format:

  1. Slide 1: Team Name, Problem Statement ID & Title, Institute Name.
  2. Slide 2: Proposed Solution Brief & Core Value Proposition.
  3. Slide 3: Technical Architecture, Flowchart & Tech Stack.
  4. Slide 4: Feasibility, User Persona & Real-World Use Case.
  5. Slide 5: Research, Novelty & Competitive Advantage.
  6. Slide 6: Implementation Roadmap & Milestones.
  7. Slide 7: Team Composition, Branch & Skill Allocation.
